# App Review notes

---

No account, login or network connection is required.

SCREENSHOTS (re Guideline 2.3.7 on the previous submission): every screenshot has been replaced. None mentions a price, "free", a discount or subscription terms; prices appear only in the description.

WHAT IS NEW: widgets (Sight Mark with interactive plus/minus, Lock Screen, Sight Tape, Last Round), a Live Activity while scoring a round, Siri / App Shortcuts ("What's my sight mark in Quiver", "Shoot a round in Quiver"), a Control Center control (iOS 18), monthly challenges and personal bests on the Range tab, the Quiver Plus subscription, two new inks (Carbon, Marsh). See them all from Sight tape tab > "Marks on your Lock Screen".

QUIVER PLUS (auto-renewable subscription group "Quiver Plus": monthly $2.99, yearly $14.99 with a 1 week free trial):
- Where: Sight tape tab > Quiver Plus card > "SEE PLUS"; also the frosted Insights card on the Range tab, the bonus challenge row, and "Or try Quiver Plus" on the Pro sheet.
- The paywall shows each plan's price and period, the free trial terms, the auto-renew terms, Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Privacy Policy links, Restore Purchases, Manage subscription and Redeem code.
- Plus includes everything in Pro, plus: Insights (Range tab), iCloud backup and restore (Sight tape tab > Quiver Plus card, uses iCloud key-value storage in the user's own iCloud), every ink while subscribed, 5 scorecard poster credits each month, and a bonus monthly challenge.
- Quiver Pro remains a one-time purchase and is still sold; Pro owners keep Pro.

OTHER IN-APP PURCHASES (StoreKit 2, Restore in Sight tape > Inks and extras and on the Pro and Plus cards):
- Quiver Pro (existing non-consumable): unlimited printed tapes, more setups, the drop chart, score trends, CSV export, the Sight Tape widget.
- One Tape Print, $0.99 consumable: Sight tape tab > "Print the tape at true size" > "Just this tape: print it once" at the foot of the Pro sheet.
- Scorecard Posters, $0.99 consumable, three credits: Range tab > open a round > Share > Poster. The first poster is free.
- Blaze, Navy, Oxblood, Timber, Carbon and Marsh inks, $0.99 each, non-consumable: Sight tape tab > "Inks and extras". A bought ink recolours the app and switches the app icon.

HOW TO TRY THE RANGE: Range tab > Shoot a round > Practice > Start. Tap the face six times, Next end, tap a few more, Finish. A Live Activity shows the round on the Lock Screen while scoring. Tap the round in the list to see the group and the sight correction.

PRIVACY: no data is collected by the developer. Rounds, setups and notes are stored on the device; Plus subscribers' notebook is also backed up to their own iCloud key-value store. No accounts, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
An archery notebook for target archers and bowhunters: arrow build (weight, FOC, energy, spine), speed and drop, a fitted sight tape that prints at true size, and a score book. Rated 4+.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup or credentials. A fresh install starts with sample numbers on each page.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None besides Apple's StoreKit and iCloud key-value storage. Built with SwiftUI, WidgetKit, ActivityKit, App Intents and TipKit.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not applicable. All art, text and code are my own work. Round names describe common scoring formats; no logos or federation artwork are used.
