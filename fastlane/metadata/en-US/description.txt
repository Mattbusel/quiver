Quiver is an archer's field notebook: the arrow build sheet and a sight tape that prints at true size.

Sight tape software costs a subscription or comes bundled with things you do not need. Quiver is the two jobs that matter, on kraft paper in your pocket.

THE ARROW
• Shaft, point, insert, nock, vanes, wrap: total weight, balance point and FOC, drawn on the page
• Kinetic energy and momentum, checked against game-weight guidelines
• A spine starting point from draw weight, point weight, length and release

BOW AND SPEED
• Speed estimated from rated speed, draw and arrow weight, or your chronograph reading
• Drag from the arrow's diameter, weight and vanes
• Drop from a 20-yard zero, time of flight and crosswind drift by distance

SIGHT TAPE
• Enter two or more marks you shot and the tape is fitted through them with the modelled trajectory, so the far end is right, not just the middle
• mm, half-mm, inch and click scales, yards or metres, your label spacing and width
• Shown at true size on screen; share as a PDF that prints 1:1, with a 10 mm check bar

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
