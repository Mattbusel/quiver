Sight tape software costs a subscription or comes bundled with things you do not need. Quiver is the two jobs that matter, on kraft paper in your pocket. Free to download, and the arrow sheet, speed and fitted marks are free forever.

THE ARROW

• Kinetic energy and momentum, checked against game-weight guidelines
• A spine starting point from draw weight, point weight, length and release

BOW AND SPEED
• Speed estimated from rated speed, draw and arrow weight, or your chronograph reading
